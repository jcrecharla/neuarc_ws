The Advancement Engine

  • Advanced Database Mastering: Connection pooling, column-store vs. row-store architectures, partition keys, and query optimisation in PostgreSQL and MongoDB

  • Whiteboard Defence: Defend your data warehousing architecture choices against AI-simulated scale constraints before our engineering review board
